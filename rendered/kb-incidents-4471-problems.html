The three problems

parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. Every tenant on file writes the day first, so 7 April comes out as 4 July. Day and month swap places; the body names both dates.

hint: booking-v3’s form tells people to type month first. Its date field prints MM/DD/YYYY, and the form then reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. People who follow the hint get the same swap, but the parser fix does not touch it: the hint and the form disagree. Only reports sent through the form of a booking-v3 tenant (Alder Physio, Copper Studio, Pale Harbour) have this cause.

one-day: the date moves by one day, it does not swap. “Tuesday 12 May. Calendar says Monday 11 May.” “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.” “The invite arrived before I made the booking.” A flipped day and month cannot do that; a time zone or a day boundary can. The desk already read these as a one-day move (desk-notes.json), then filed them under parser by tenant.

The fix queued for #4471 closes parser only. hint and one-day stay open until they have their own fix.

Why one ticket held all three

Decision 0001 merges on subject alone. All three problems arrive as “Wrong day on the invite” or “Month and day swapped?”, so they all landed in one ticket. The intake tag does not separate them either: it is guessed from the reporter’s browser, not their tenant, and it often disagrees with the cause.
