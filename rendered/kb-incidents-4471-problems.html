The two problems

parser: the shared parser read every date month first. Every tenant on file writes the day first, so 7 April came out as 4 July. Fixed: fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales.

hint: booking-v3’s form told people to type month first. Its date field printed MM/DD/YYYY, and the form then read the date the tenant’s way, day first. People who followed the hint got the same swap, and the parser fix did not touch it. Only reports sent through the form of a booking-v3 tenant have this cause. Won’t fix: the booking-v3 form is retired.

Some parser reports describe a date that moved by one day, not a swap ("Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday."). The desk counts them under parser, where they are filed. If reports like these still come in after the parser fix, they are a problem of their own: open a new ticket.

Why one ticket held both

Decision 0001 merges on subject alone. Both problems arrive as “Wrong day on the invite” or “Month and day swapped?”, so they landed in one ticket. The intake tag does not separate them either: it is guessed from the reporter’s browser, not their tenant’s form. The table above counts by tenant and form, and counts each report id once.
